# Changelog

Substantive revisions to the framework, as [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) asks. Each entry names what was added, which documents it reached and how the added claims are labelled. Objections that changed the text are recorded with the change that answered them.

### Unreleased: Research extensions

Three provisional research tracks beside the framework, each in its own directory with its own entry point. They specialise the commitments of the nine documents; they do not amend them; the nine remain the framework.

- `linguistic-evolution/` (eight files): language as a culturally evolving system, archival stewardship (archived means preserved and recoverable, never destroyed), governance safeguards against linguistic authoritarianism, a bounded case study of Nazi euphemism, a research programme with six pilots and sources.
- `civilisational-resilience/` (six files): distributing protective capability without eugenics or reproductive control, reproductive autonomy and child rights, the earlier-life high-threat hypothetical held as a speculative scenario, governance pilots and sources.
- `governance-integrity/` (nine files): fallibility and correction, reciprocal oversight, benevolent strength and restraint, tragic choices, evidence and civilisational time, the courage to care, what counts as winning, research and adversarial tests.
- `care-and-influence/` (four files), a companion to the governance track linked from its index: benevolence that is never seen or credited, intellectual influence without personal acquaintance, gratitude as voluntary rather than a debt, the Greek love vocabulary held as useful distinctions rather than a fixed taxonomy, a research programme with a threat model.
- `iul-mars/` (one document): the Integration Up Level sequence, a repeated-integration discipline in which every constant is kept and fixed only by a stated condition, applied to a coupled Martian inventory ledger beginning with carbon dioxide electrolysis; the three scenarios (enclosed, regional, global) kept distinct, the global one held as a long-horizon research scenario with the published feasibility limits stated; the ethical, scientific and institutional gates before any intervention; TrellisDB as the experimental store; a development sequence M0 to M5.
- `plural-rooting/` (one document): continuity without subordination, independence without forgetting, cooperation without a single root. Historical origin, present authority, ongoing relationships and shared achievements kept as separate relations in a sketched graph; re-rooting as a transition that changes active authority while the archive keeps the earlier root and the dissent; the root, rout, route, tour vocabulary stated as a mnemonic and nothing more; five historical illustrations checked at source (Boston 1773, the Declaration 1776, Paris 1783; the later British-American relationship; the Entente Cordiale 1904; the Statue of Liberty 1886; the Eiffel Tower 1889); an Earth-and-Mars case beside the IUL extension; ten tests with failure conditions. Eight glossary terms, two Workstream 2 items and a red-team question in RESEARCH.md, one cross-link each from ARCHITECTS.md, MEMORY.md and iul-mars.
- [README.md](README.md) items 10 to 14 and a root [START_HERE.md](START_HERE.md) link the tracks; the site carries a section for them.

### 0.2.0: Incompleteness

- [MATHEMATICS.md](MATHEMATICS.md) gains section 9, Gödel's incompleteness theorems: the two theorems stated for the systems they concern; three consequences for the transition model, labelled mathematical result, definition and hypothesis; a statement of what incompleteness does not say; the consequence for the viability statement. The former section 9 becomes section 10 and gains a bullet on labelling analogies.
- [VISION.md](VISION.md): the viability statement carries the structural caution that no fixed formal system should be expected to decide it, so the capacity to extend the theory is part of the ambition (hypothesis). "A complete and final theory of viability" joins the list of what is not proposed.
- [ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md), [ARCHITECTS.md](ARCHITECTS.md), [MEMORY.md](MEMORY.md): one paragraph each, labelled speculation by analogy, on why a code, an institution and a civilisation's own record cannot be self-certifying.
- [COSMOLOGY.md](COSMOLOGY.md): Hawking's 2002 argument that incompleteness may bear on a theory of everything, recorded as a hypothesis about physical theories with no bearing on heat death.
- [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md): Workstream 1 asks what viability can mean under incompleteness; Workstream 4 gains the axiom catalogue and the extension step; milestone M3 gains the catalogue; a red-team question asks whether incompleteness is being used as a licence for vagueness; a roadmap names this release and the planned 0.3.0.
- [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md): consistency, decidability, formal system, incompleteness theorems.
- [README.md](README.md): the reading list, the epistemic boundaries and the contribution section updated; `VERSION` introduced as the single source of the version.
