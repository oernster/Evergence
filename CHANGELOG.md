# Changelog

Substantive revisions to the framework, as [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) asks. Each entry names what was added, which documents it reached and how the added claims are labelled. Objections that changed the text are recorded with the change that answered them.

### 0.2.0: Incompleteness

- [MATHEMATICS.md](MATHEMATICS.md) gains section 9, Gödel's incompleteness theorems: the two theorems stated for the systems they concern; three consequences for the transition model, labelled mathematical result, definition and hypothesis; a statement of what incompleteness does not say; the consequence for the viability statement. The former section 9 becomes section 10 and gains a bullet on labelling analogies.
- [VISION.md](VISION.md): the viability statement carries the structural caution that no fixed formal system should be expected to decide it, so the capacity to extend the theory is part of the ambition (hypothesis). "A complete and final theory of viability" joins the list of what is not proposed.
- [ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md), [ARCHITECTS.md](ARCHITECTS.md), [MEMORY.md](MEMORY.md): one paragraph each, labelled speculation by analogy, on why a code, an institution and a civilisation's own record cannot be self-certifying.
- [COSMOLOGY.md](COSMOLOGY.md): Hawking's 2002 argument that incompleteness may bear on a theory of everything, recorded as a hypothesis about physical theories with no bearing on heat death.
- [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md): Workstream 1 asks what viability can mean under incompleteness; Workstream 4 gains the axiom catalogue and the extension step; milestone M3 gains the catalogue; a red-team question asks whether incompleteness is being used as a licence for vagueness; a roadmap names this release and the planned 0.3.0.
- [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md): consistency, decidability, formal system, incompleteness theorems.
- [README.md](README.md): the reading list, the epistemic boundaries and the contribution section updated; `VERSION` introduced as the single source of the version.
