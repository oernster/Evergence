## What is in this extension package?

1. **[Linguistic evolution and archival stewardship](linguistic-evolution/START_HERE.md)**: the proposed general mechanism for all human languages, a falsifiable research programme, preservation without erasure and anti-authoritarian safeguards.
2. **[Civilisational capability distribution and non-eugenic resilience](civilisational-resilience/README.md)**: a separate adjunct asking how communities can avoid dangerous shortages or concentration of protective capabilities *without reproductive control or predetermined individual worth*.
3. **[Governance integrity, courage to care and living well](governance-integrity/README.md)**: accountable power, caring intervention, fallibility and plural definitions of a good life.
4. **[Integration Up Levels and Martian atmospheric engineering](iul-mars/README.md)**: a repeated-integration discipline that keeps every constant, applied to a Mars inventory ledger with its feasibility limits and ethical gates stated.
5. **[Original Evergence framework](README.md)**: the existing project's primary starting point and authoritative commitments, including [`ETHICS.md`](ETHICS.md), [`MEMORY.md`](MEMORY.md), [`ARCHITECTS.md`](ARCHITECTS.md) and [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md).

## Common commitments

- **No deliberate destruction of historical records**, including harmful language, contested scientific work and evidence of past atrocity. Preservation, legitimate retrieval and interpretation are distinct duties.
- **No eugenics, reproductive quotas, breeding rights, coercive parent selection or classification of people by hereditary worth.**
- No entire living language is judged dangerous simply by its identity or speakers.
- No newborn or other person is presumed dangerous due to a hypothesised prior life. This has not been demonstrated as a scientifically reliable way to identify people or predict behaviour.
- Any governing body or classifier must be open to external scrutiny, appeal, independent review and reversal.
- Theory, hypothetical, evidence and policy recommendations must remain explicitly separated.
