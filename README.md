# Civilisational Continuity and Open-Ended Evolution

*A provisional philosophical, scientific and mathematical research framework.*

## Purpose

The primary ambition of this project is **the continued survival and evolution of humanity and other life**, while preserving meaningful human-descended agency and making room for other conscious entities to participate in decisions affecting their futures.

Expansion, technological advancement, additional dimensions, wealth, knowledge and even alterations to the physical environment are **means, not ends**. Survival must not become a justification for atrocity, domination, forced uniformity or compulsory stagnation.

This repository is a **starting point for inquiry, not a declaration of discovered physical laws or a final constitution for future civilisation**. Its postulates remain open to evidence, criticism and revision.

## Guiding commitments

- **Survival with evolution** is the principal ambition, not expansion for its own sake.
- Meaning is not assigned from above; people and other conscious entities may discover or invent their own.
- Life should have room for love, joy, friendship, honesty, creativity, disagreement and self-direction.
- No abstract numerical ideal of balance or optimisation justifies treating living beings as expendable.
- Humanity should retain a controlling or at least primarily influential role in its continuity, while the rights and interests of other conscious entities receive serious consideration.
- Architects should be compassionate, emotionally mature, scientifically and intellectually capable, historically informed and creatively appreciative.
- Preserve authentic records of the past, including failures and atrocities; distinguish preservation from unrestricted publication.
- Long-term decisions require informed participation, review, challenge and correction.
- Scientific hypotheses must remain distinguishable from philosophical commitments and mathematical constructions.

## Status and epistemic boundaries

**Established mathematics:** balanced ternary, Euler's formula, roots of unity, infinite product spaces, Gödel's incompleteness theorems.

**Established or evidence-supported physics:** thermodynamics, the observed expansion of the universe and the current standard cosmological model within its tested domain.

**Unsettled physics:** the ultimate fate of the universe, the nature of dark energy and whether new physics could alter long-term cosmological outcomes.

**Established economics:** scarcity and opportunity cost, present-value arithmetic, the under-provision of public goods, the thermodynamic cost of information.

**Contested economics:** the rate of time preference across generations, how tightly thermodynamic limits bind economic activity, whether growth is needed at all.

**Speculative constructions:** assigning ternary states to arbitrarily many dimensions; interpreting civilisational evolution as new degrees of freedom; reading evolutionary stages as extensions of a formal system; cosmological transitions that preserve a civilisation.

No mathematical elegance, by itself, establishes physical reality.

Begin with [VISION.md](VISION.md), then [ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md) and [ARCHITECTS.md](ARCHITECTS.md). The mathematical, cosmological and economic material provides a research language and a set of constraints, **not proof** of the vision.
