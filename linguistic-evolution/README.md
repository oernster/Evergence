# Linguistic Evolution, Civilisational Risk and Archival Stewardship

*A provisional extension to [Evergence](../README.md); research proposal, not a declaration of settled causality or a mandate to suppress languages.*

**[START HERE: a guided reading route](START_HERE.md)** · [Evergence extensions index](../START_HERE.md)

## Purpose

Evergence considers the continuing evolution of civilisation and the preservation of its historical memory. This extension asks whether **languages and their constituent conceptual structures**, transmitted and modified over generations, can sometimes help harmful ideas persist, escape scrutiny and become embedded in institutions, together with what a civilisation can responsibly do about that possibility.

The question is deliberately **universal**: it applies to every natural human language and linguistic modality, including signed, spoken, written, endangered, indigenous, creole, mixed and emergent forms. German-language euphemisms used by the Nazi regime are examined as one historical case, **not** as proof that German, its speakers or its grammar caused Nazism.

## Non-negotiable distinctions

1. **Archiving is preservation, not destruction.** An archived language remains recoverable in authentic, context-rich forms. This work rejects burning books, destroying records, deleting digital collections or deliberately making knowledge irretrievable.
2. **Archiving is not prohibiting use.** Documentation, preservation, historical classification, institutional deprecation of a specific harmful phrase, voluntary language dormancy and coercive suppression of a living language are fundamentally different actions.
3. **A language is not its speakers or a political ideology.** Risk assessments concern identifiable mechanisms in context, never the inherent moral status of an ethnic, linguistic or national community.
4. **The investigators also require investigation.** A body that judges dangerous language can itself construct and replicate authoritarian concepts; its procedures must be independent, contestable, transparent and reversible.
5. **No false certainty.** Language as a 'memeplex' or lifeform is a useful *analogy/hypothesis*, not a demonstrated biological or autonomous organism. Evidence of cultural evolution is stronger than evidence for any complete theory of memes.

## Provisional policy, not predetermined conclusion

Start with preservation and education; examine specific harmful uses and institutional practices; prefer narrower, proportionate, reversible responses with independently reviewable evidence. The **compulsory retirement of an entire living language is not justified by this proposal**. A community may voluntarily transition the everyday use of a language while preserving it; no archival label should extinguish linguistic rights.

A proposed change to any language practice must satisfy the test in [GOVERNANCE_SAFEGUARDS.md](GOVERNANCE_SAFEGUARDS.md) before adoption. Unproven risk should lead to further study, not coercive action.
