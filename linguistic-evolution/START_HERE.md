## The question

If human languages and recurring conceptual constructs undergo cultural evolution, under what circumstances can linguistic compression, semantic drift or institutional repetition conceal moral implications and assist destructive ideologies in spreading? Can these mechanisms be understood, taught about and addressed **without destroying evidence or suppressing entire living languages**?

## First principles

1. The unit of analysis is **a documented construct in its context**, never a nationality, ethnic community or a whole language judged inherently dangerous.
2. Archives preserve source materials, provenance, contested interpretations and recoverability. **Archive does not mean erase or ban.**
3. A mechanism hypothesis is not evidence that a particular language caused historical atrocities.
4. Independent institutions must check for political capture and allow appeals.
