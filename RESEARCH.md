# Research Programme and Open Questions

[← README](README.md) · [Economics](ECONOMICS.md) · [Glossary →](GLOSSARY.md)

## Research ethos

Treat the survival ambition seriously while maintaining strict distinctions among values, definitions, mathematics, simulations, hypotheses and experimentally supported claims. Seek criticism early. No architect or theory is exempt from revision.

## Workstream 1: Define the objective

- What qualifies as continued humanity: biological lineage, cultural continuity, consciousness, institutions or combinations?
- How can human-descended influence be maintained without subordinating other conscious life?
- How can flourishing be evaluated without imposing one universal purpose?
- What does 'evolution' mean when direction and goals may change?
- What can 'viability' mean when no fixed formal system decides it for every future time ([MATHEMATICS.md](MATHEMATICS.md) section 9)?

**Output:** operational definitions, alternative formulations and documented disagreements.

## Workstream 2: Ethical and institutional safeguards

- Design participatory processes for living, future and non-human stakeholders.
- Identify unacceptable means of pursuing survival.
- Model conflicts among survival, autonomy, privacy, biodiversity and justice.
- Design selection, oversight, correction and removal procedures for architects.
- Test proposals against coercive or catastrophic optimisation failures.
- Test whether a record of authority can keep historical origin, present authority, ongoing relationships and shared achievements as separate relations, so that ancestry never reads as authority and independence never reads as justice; the ten tests and the failure conditions are in [plural rooting](plural-rooting/README.md).
- Test whether a re-rooting (a change of sovereignty root) can be represented with the earlier authority, the transition and the dissent all still recoverable from the archive.

**Output:** a revisable governance charter with explicit dissent and appeal mechanisms.

## Workstream 3: Civilisational memory

- Specify provenance, authenticity, replication and disaster recovery.
- Separate preservation, access and interpretation.
- Design proportionate access restrictions and independent review.
- Test archive durability against institutional collapse and technological change.
- Reconcile historical preservation with privacy and protection of victims.

**Output:** archival requirements, threat models and demonstrable restoration tests.

## Workstream 4: Mathematical model

- Formalise stage-dependent state spaces and transition operators.
- Decide whether ternary states encode decisions, physical states or abstract degrees of freedom.
- Compare balanced ternary with binary, continuous and other representations.
- Explore coupled phase models, information preservation and path dependence.
- State the model's axioms explicitly; catalogue which questions about trajectories are undecidable within them (Gödel, [MATHEMATICS.md](MATHEMATICS.md) section 9).
- Define the extension step: whether \(\Delta D_n\) corresponds to added axioms and whether bounded-horizon viability can be proved stage by stage.
- Identify observable consequences; abandon formalisms that add no explanatory power.

**Output:** explicit definitions, proofs where possible, simulations and counterexamples.

## Workstream 5: Cosmological survival

- Review long-term cosmological models and their observational constraints.
- Examine dark-energy scenarios and entropy in expanding spacetimes.
- Investigate resource and computation limits for arbitrarily long-lived systems.
- Evaluate speculative transitions for causality, energy and continuity.
- Separate survival extension from demonstrated indefinite survival.

**Output:** evidence-ranked hypotheses and falsifiable predictions wherever possible.

## Workstream 6: Economics of continuity

- Argue the rate of time preference across civilisational stages as a normative commitment; record the alternatives and what each implies for the stage after next.
- Replace the toy budget in [ECONOMICS.md](ECONOMICS.md) with a model carrying stated units, floors for survival and memory and an explicit link from evolution spending to later supply.
- Test Ostrom's design principles as candidate safeguards for architect institutions; identify which fail at civilisational scale and why.
- Audit every measure the framework proposes for its Goodhart failure: what is gamed when the measure becomes a target.
- Cost the three duties of memory (preservation, access, interpretation) against the thermodynamic floor on information.
- Examine whether any growth assumption has entered the framework through economic vocabulary.

**Output:** a declared and argued discount commitment, a costed budget model, a Goodhart audit of every proposed measure.

## Red-team questions

- Does this framework secretly optimise expansion rather than life?
- Could an architect use 'survival' to justify atrocities?
- Could archival controls become censorship?
- Are non-human interests merely symbolic?
- Are 'dimensions' being confused with physical spacetime?
- Does the model produce predictions or only metaphors?
- Can a future generation reject inherited values without losing access to inherited evidence?
- Is incompleteness being invoked as a licence for vagueness, mysticism or unfalsifiable claims?
- Has a discount rate been applied anywhere without being argued?
- Is economic vocabulary carrying a growth imperative back in?
- Does any representation of authority let historical origin stand in for present legitimacy or let agreement between two governments stand in for the consent of everyone affected?

## Roadmap

Each release adds one evaluation and integrates it through every document it touches. The record of what changed is [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md); the current version is in `VERSION`.

- **0.2.0:** Gödel's incompleteness theorems, stated in [MATHEMATICS.md](MATHEMATICS.md) section 9 and carried as a labelled caution into the vision, ethics, architects, memory and cosmology documents.
- **0.3.0, this release:** modern economic theory as a constraint set, in [ECONOMICS.md](ECONOMICS.md): scarcity, discounting as a normative decision, entropy economics, intergenerational public goods and the commons record, incentives and Goodhart, the thermodynamic cost of memory, a toy budget. Carried into the vision, ethics, architects, memory, mathematics and cosmology documents, with Workstream 6 and milestone M7 here.
- **Next evaluation:** not yet chosen. Candidates are recorded as objections arrive.

## Contribution conventions

Label claims as **normative**, **definition**, **mathematical result**, **empirical evidence**, **hypothesis** or **speculation**. Cite primary research for physical claims. Record substantive revisions and objections in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
